Before you call

Anything else, ring us on 09 439 5150.

What vehicles do you work on?

Cars, utes and tractors. If you are not sure whether we can help with your vehicle, give us a call and ask.

Do you fit bullbars and winches?

Yes. We do bullbars, winches and 4WD accessories. Call us with your make and model and we can talk you through the options.

Do you do auto electrical work?

Yes, including batteries and electrical faults. Tell us what is happening when you call and we will get it booked in.

How do I book my vehicle in?

Call us on 09 439 5150 or email us using the button below, and we will find a time that works.

Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm. We are closed on weekends.
